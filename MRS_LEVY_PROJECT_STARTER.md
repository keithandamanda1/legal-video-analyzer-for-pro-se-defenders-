## LEGAL GROUNDS TO CHALLENGE

1. **Statute of limitations** — 36 M.R.S. § 176-A(16): 10-year collection limit. Original assessments: 2013–2014 → limit expired 2023–2024. Entire levy may be time-barred.
2. **Mathematical impossibility** — Their own 2016 notice shows $205.75 balance. Cannot legally be $6,073.74+ now.
3. **Wrong account type** — Loan proceeds are not subject to levy under Maine law.
4. **ADA accommodation violation** — Written-only communication accommodation requested and documented March 9, 2026. Every phone contact after that date is a violation.
