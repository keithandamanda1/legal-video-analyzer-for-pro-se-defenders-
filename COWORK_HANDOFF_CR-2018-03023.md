4. **Warrant timeline impossibility** — Warrant signed at 12:00 PM. Officer Farrar (affiant) did not arrive at residence until 12:56 PM — 56 minutes AFTER signing. She could not have personally observed what she swore to. CAD Log confirms.

7. **Knock and announce violated** — Warrant did NOT check "Unannounced Execution" box. Entry was unannounced. Keith asked to see warrant twice. Officers said "We will get to that." ON VIDEO.

11. **TruNarc test: INCONCLUSIVE** — No confirmatory lab analysis. "In fact heroin" charge had no scientific basis.
