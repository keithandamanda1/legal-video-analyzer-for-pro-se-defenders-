                          PRINT & SIGN PHASE
================================================================================

[ ] 1. PRINT THE PETITION

    a. Use PCR_PETITION_CR-2018-03023_PDF.html for best results

       METHOD 1: Browser Print
       - Open PCR_PETITION_CR-2018-03023_PDF.html in Google Chrome or Firefox
       - Press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac)
       - Select "Print to PDF" or your default printer
       - Verify: margins are 1 inch all sides, font is 12pt, double-spaced
       - If printing to PDF: Save as "PCR_PETITION_CR-2018-03023_ORIGINAL.pdf"
       - If printing to paper: Use standard 8.5" x 11" white paper (20lb weight)

       METHOD 2: Text Version for Manual Formatting
       - Use PCR_PETITION_CR-2018-03023_FORMATTED.txt
       - Open in Microsoft Word or Google Docs
       - Import, ensuring margins are 1" all sides
       - Font: 12-point Times New Roman, double-spaced
       - Print to standard paper

[ ] 2. SIGN THE PETITION (CRITICAL — MUST BE ORIGINAL)

    a. Use BLACK OR BLUE INK ONLY (no pencil, no photocopied signature)

    b. Sign on Page 3 (or final page) where it says:
       "____________________________
        Keith A. King, Pro Se"

    c. Print your name underneath the signature line

    d. Sign again on the Certificate of Service page where indicated

    e. DATE each signature: April 13, 2026 (or current filing date if different)

    NOTE: Maine courts require ORIGINAL signatures on legal pleadings.
          Photocopies, faxes, and electronic signatures may not be accepted
          for pro se filings. Do not sign in pencil.

[ ] 3. MAKE COPIES

    a. After signing the original:
       - Make 2 COMPLETE photocopies of the signed original
       - Total: 1 original + 2 copies = 3 sets
       - Verify each copy is legible, especially the signature

    b. Organize each set:
       - Original: Keep in secure location (you will file this)
       - Copy #1: For District Attorney (you will serve this)
       - Copy #2: Keep for your records

    c. Staple each set in upper left corner


================================================================================
                        FILING WITH THE COURT
================================================================================

[ ] 4. PREPARE FOR COURT FILING

    Court: Penobscot Unified District Court
    Address: 97 State Street
             Bangor, ME 04401
    Phone: (207) 942-8500
    Hours: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM

    What to Bring:
    - Original signed petition (3+ pages, stapled)
    - 1 certified copy (for court)
    - 1 certified copy (for District Attorney's file)
    - Filing fee (if applicable — see below)

[ ] 5. FILE THE PETITION WITH THE COURT

    a. Call ahead (207) 942-8500 to confirm:
       - Filing window (typically 8 AM – 4 PM)
       - Whether filing fees apply (pro se may have waiver available)
       - Current filing procedures (electronic vs. in-person)

    b. In-Person Filing (Preferred for Pro Se):

       Step 1: Arrive at Penobscot Unified District Court
               97 State Street, Bangor, ME 04401

       Step 2: Proceed to Criminal Division clerk's office

       Step 3: Tell clerk: "I'm filing a Petition for Post-Conviction Review
               under 15 M.R.S. §§ 2121–2130-A for docket CR-2018-03023"

       Step 4: Provide:
               - Original petition (signed)
               - 1 copy for the court file
               - Filing fee (or IFP motion if unable to pay)

       Step 5: The clerk will:
               - Stamp receipt or file-stamp copies
               - Record docket entry
               - Provide you with filing receipt and date

       Step 6: Request written proof of filing with date/time

[ ] 6. CERTIFIED MAILING TO DISTRICT ATTORNEY

    After filing with court:

    a. Prepare certified copies for DA:
       - 1 copy of the signed petition
       - 1 cover letter (see template below)

    b. Send via USPS Certified Mail to:

       District Attorney
       Penobscot County District Attorney's Office
       97 State Street
       Bangor, ME 04401

    c. Use Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested
       - Cost: ~$8 + postage
       - Keep the green return receipt (proof of service)
       - Note the tracking number and date mailed

    d. Alternative: Hand-deliver copy to DA's office
       - Ask clerk to stamp "Served" with date and time
       - Keep stamped copy for your records

[ ] 7. COMPLETE THE CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE

    After serving the District Attorney, complete the final section:

    On the last page of your original filed petition, you should have:

    "I certify that I have served a true copy of this Petition for
    Post-Conviction Review on the District Attorney for Penobscot County by
    [SELECT ONE]: ☒ Hand delivery  ☐ Certified mail  ☐ Electronic filing

    Date: _________ (the date you served the DA)

    _______________________
    Keith A. King, Pro Se"

    File this completed certificate with the court within 10 days.

================================================================================
                       CRITICAL REMINDERS
================================================================================

⚠ ORIGINAL SIGNATURES REQUIRED
  Do not file copies or faxes of signatures. Use original ink signatures.

⚠ SERVE THE DISTRICT ATTORNEY
  Failure to serve the DA may result in petition being rejected.
  Keep proof of service (certified mail receipt or hand-delivery stamp).

⚠ FILE PROOF OF SERVICE
  Complete the Certificate of Service and file it with the court
  within 10 days of service.

⚠ KEEP COPIES FOR YOURSELF
  Retain copies of everything you file. The court works with originals;
  you need your own records.

⚠ FOLLOW UP IF NO RESPONSE
  If you don't receive an acknowledgment within 2 weeks, call the clerk
  to confirm the petition was received.

⚠ ATTEND ALL HEARINGS
  Even if you have counsel, attend all scheduled hearings and oral arguments.
  Your presence demonstrates commitment to your case.
