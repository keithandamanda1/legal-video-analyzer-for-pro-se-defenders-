FINDING 3 — PHYSICAL PAT-DOWN / HANDS-ON CONTROL OF SUBJECT AT REAR OF
VEHICLE (CERTAIN — confirmed across 5 consecutive frames)
Timestamps: 00:03:20, 00:03:40, 00:03:50, 00:04:00 (continuous sequence)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Across four sampled frames spanning 40 seconds, the officer is in continuous physical
contact with the subject at the rear of the stopped vehicle: hands on the subject's
body in pat-down position (00:03:20, 00:03:40), subject's arm positioning consistent
with a control hold (00:03:50), continuing at 00:04:00. One frame could be ambiguous;
five in sequence are not.

What this proves with certainty:
  • A hands-on physical search/frisk of the subject's body occurred.
  • The subject was physically detained (seized) at the rear of the vehicle.

What it does NOT prove by itself: whether the frisk was legally justified. A Terry
frisk requires reasonable articulable suspicion the person is ARMED AND DANGEROUS —
that justification must appear in the officer's report, or the frisk was unlawful.

Law: Terry v. Ohio, 392 U.S. 1 (1968); 4th Amendment; Maine Const. Art. I §5.
